AgentCo

A private assistant for one person.

AgentCo is a personal tool built for and used by a single owner. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

The owner gave AgentCo its own Gmail account. With the owner’s permission, AgentCo uses that account to:

  • read messages, to summarize them and find what needs attention
  • write draft replies, which the owner reviews

AgentCo never sends an email on its own. Every message is approved by the owner first.
